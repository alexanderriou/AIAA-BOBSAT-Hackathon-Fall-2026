Bobcat Ballistics requires the contracted team to produce orbital simulations using Ansys STK to demonstrate the satellite's ability to sense areas of interest, communicate with the ground station, and remain power positive while in mission. 

For this deliverable, the analysts shall deliver the following:
1. An STK animation showing the satellite, the ground trace, and orbital path in 2D and 3D from X, Y, 202z to X2, Y2, 202z.
2. An access report between the ground station located on top of UC Merced's SRE (37.36451, -120.42446, 0.037km) and the satellite.
3. A simple coverage analysis over X, demonstrating coverage over the area of interest by the satellite's rectangular sensor (5 deg x 10 deg).

For further consideration, the analysts should deliver the following:
1.  A parameterized trade study showing how modifying orbital parameters increase coverage over area of interest.
2.  A coverage analysis constrained by direct sunlight during the sensor's observation.
3.  An access report constrained by an AzEl mask using the terrain present at or around the ground station's location.
